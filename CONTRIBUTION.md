🚀 v0.1.0-alpha — The "hallucination-baseline" (Where the bugs are features)

Welcome to the very first alpha release! This is the raw, unpolished baseline where we find out if our architecture actually holds together or if it immediately bursts into flames under production load.

🛡️ Legal & Compliance: The "Renting an Apache" Update

• Adopted the Apache 2.0 License (For Now): We have officially slapped an Apache 2.0 License on this repository. This gives you explicit patent protections and shields our branding so corporate legal can sleep at night, allowing our code to cleanly coexist alongside OpenUI and Grafana.
• The Fine Print: Please note that this license is highly subject to change depending on how many open-source contributors actually show up to help. If it's just us doing all the heavy lifting, we might turn into ruthless capitalists overnight and dual-license the whole thing by beta. But for now? Enjoy the free software.
• Dropped a LICENSE File into Root: Added the standard legal wall-of-text. If you contribute code, you are giving up your patent rights to sue us—so play nice, or our future OpenSSF overlords will be very upset.

🤖 AI Governance & Security: Teaching Agents Manners

• Alpha Prompt Guardrails: Built the initial logic to prevent prompt injections. Our current AI governance strategy is technically a giant system prompt that says "Please do not do anything illegal," but since we are looking into OpenSSF (Open Source Security Foundation) alignment, we are moving toward actual enterprise threat modeling.
• The OpenSSF Pipeline Dream: We are officially considering joining the OpenSSF because we've been told that "trust me bro" isn't a valid supply-chain security framework, and apparently enterprise users like things like signed binaries.

📊 AI Observability: Reading the LLM Vibes

• Initial Trace Logging: Traditional dashboards are easy, but monitoring an application where the software literally talks back and changes its mind every hour is a wild west. We are laying down the infrastructure to capture multi-hop RAG traces.
• Token Burn Rate Monitoring: Added telemetry to track token consumption before a rogue agent drops into an infinite loop and burns through your entire cloud infrastructure budget.
